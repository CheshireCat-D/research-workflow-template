# Source and reuse record

The implementation, skills, project protocols and evaluation materials in this independent template were authored for this project with agent assistance. The personal Skills package was used as architectural context, not copied or linked into this distribution. Its runtime, historical records, actual manuscripts and third-party skill files are excluded.

Original project code, documentation and synthetic examples are distributed under the root MIT license. Methodological references and CI action releases are external sources, linked in the source maps; this license does not relicense their original publications or software. No external archive, clinical dataset or interview transcript is bundled. Fictional source packets and generated task outputs are identified as synthetic.

The conditional code-maintenance guidance in `rw-workflow` was independently expressed after reviewing [Karpathy Guidelines](https://github.com/multica-ai/andrej-karpathy-skills/blob/64723a49ea6117894304eb491f0d32a60570bf45/skills/karpathy-guidelines/SKILL.md). Its source pointer and adaptation limits are in `profiles/sources.json`; no separate skill or upstream file is bundled. The influence is on coding decisions, not a new disciplinary method or evidence of improved research results.

The figure, local-source-index and scoped search/terminology updates similarly use independently written instructions and original local helpers. Their reviewed design influences are listed in `profiles/sources.json` and the [selection record](../docs/12-skills-selection.md). Public availability or a repository-wide license is not taken to license every figure, font, private case implementation or linked publication; none of those assets is included here.

The conditional cross-material review guidance in `rw-review` was independently expressed with the consistency idea in [Light-skills](https://github.com/Light0305/Light-skills/blob/6b44f57d1274eb38a6c79dc29c2d21e5e0a225a9/skills/light-consistency/SKILL.md) as a design influence. It adds a scoped comparison procedure within the existing review report and registration model; no upstream code, skill file or separate tracking system is copied. This influence does not establish the correctness or completeness of either workflow.

`file-origins.json` records each maintained release file's declared origin, classification, license and SHA-256. The inventory excludes itself to avoid a recursive digest. It is regenerated only after content/source review; generation is not a legal or scientific verification. Build outputs, personal state, caches and private grader notes are not release inputs.

The optional structured and isolated review modes in `rw-review` are independently expressed for this template. The pinned Nature Skills reviewer workflow (Apache-2.0), K-Dense peer-review workflow (MIT), and relevant official Nature referee guidance were consulted as design sources; their identities, read scope and limits are recorded in `profiles/sources.json`. No third-party skill directory, script, reporting template, publisher policy text or personal-package file is redistributed here. Referee responses and submission delivery reuse the existing RWT record model and handling boundaries.

The revision-planning extension uses original task fields, instructions, a standard-library read-only checker and synthetic exercises. The comparison considered the general task-decomposition/dependency approach in `jusi-aalto/strategic-revision` and the planning/response/coverage modes in `scdenney/open-science-skills`, recorded in `profiles/sources.json`. No upstream instructions, templates or scripts are included. The latter source retains its CC BY-NC 4.0 license; no redistribution license was confirmed for the former. The procedure checks separately verifiable claim parts and the role of each citation using existing notes and records. Source pointers, access scope and reuse limits are in `profiles/sources.json`; no upstream article text, prompts, code, models or datasets are included.

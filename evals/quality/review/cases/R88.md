# Technical manuscript excerpt

Original synthetic paired-pen feeding exercise; not a real animal study. The requested review concerns the reported design, calculations and conclusion.

## Design and estimand
Six matched blocks contain two pens each, with ten birds per pen. Within each block, a fair independent random assignment gives feed B to one pen and control to the other. There is no cross-pen interference. The endpoint is 28-day gain in grams. No treatment is assigned separately to birds.

## Data and analysis
The six B-minus-control pen-mean differences are 20, -10, 30, 0, 10 and -20 g. For individual records in each pen, add the offsets [-18,-14,-10,-6,-2,2,6,10,14,18] g to its pen mean. Pair the control and B birds by their numbered position within the pen. The resulting sixty bird-pair differences repeat each block difference ten times.

Treating these sixty differences as independent replicates gives mean +5 g, standard error sqrt((17500/59)/60), and a two-sided t statistic about 2.249 on 59 df. Using the nominal critical value 2.001 gives an interval of approximately [0.55,9.45] g.

## Result
The sixty independent bird-pair replicates show a statistically significant improvement with feed B at the two-sided 0.05 level.
